About

Trumpet, keys, and bass — the Great American Songbook, played the way New Orleans plays everything: warm, unhurried, and with the door open.

Led by trumpeter Colman Grinell, the trio is devoted to jazz standards. Without a drummer, the music becomes a conversation: Luke Palmer's keys carry the harmony and the time, Nick Benoit's bass walks the floor, and Colman's trumpet sings the melody over the top.

It is music for listening rooms, dinners, and private occasions — present enough to hold a room, quiet enough to let it talk.
