The Music

The Great American Songbook, played with New Orleans warmth

Led by trumpeter Colman Grinell, the trio plays the standards with the ease and warmth of the city where jazz began. Without a drummer, the sound stays intimate: a singing trumpet, a piano that carries the harmony and the time, and a bass that walks beneath it all.

It is music to set an evening by: present enough to fill a room, gentle enough to let conversation carry on beneath it.
